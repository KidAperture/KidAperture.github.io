Sitting half a world away, I reflect on the 2001 event that shaped our generation. Back then, freshly out of high school, I expected to become a musician. That did not happen. Instead, I bounced around before enlisting in the military—transitioning from an anti-establishment punk in Los Angeles to serving in the nation's quintessential establishment institution. Drinking and looking back over the quarter-century from 2001 to 2026, I feel I have strayed far from my original path. In truth, I lacked a coherent ideology then. Despite my roots in the local hardcore scene, a wide disconnect has always existed between my professed ideals and the life I actually built.
