There is a unique stillness to be found in the transit systems of Los Angeles, especially when you catch a car at the right time.

This shot was captured on the **LA Metro Green Line**, heading out from Norwalk. In the blur of the commute, the architecture of the subway car takes on a different form—symmetrical, quiet, and caught in that fleeting moment of transit between destinations.


### Technical Details
Capturing this scene required a slow, intentional approach. I shot this on my **Canon F-1**, a workhorse of a camera that feels perfectly suited for this kind of candid, mechanical observation. There’s something about the grain and contrast of film that captures the cool, industrial atmosphere of the Green Line better than any digital sensor could.

The Green Line has always felt like a different world compared to the bustling, subterranean Red or Purple lines. It’s elevated, exposed to the light of the city, and often quieter.

*Photography captured on 35mm film.*
